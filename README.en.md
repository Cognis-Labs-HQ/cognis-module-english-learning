# English Study

Study English writing, words and sentences using a language pack inside Cognis Study.

## What You Can Do

Browse English study layers, inspect related entries, practise with starter decks and listen to available pronunciation recordings.

## Before You Start

An administrator must enable the language pack. Sign in and enroll in English through Study; dictionary and optional speech services require their own configuration.

## Get Started

Select English in Study, open Explore and choose a layer. Open an entry to inspect its readings, meaning and related content; use Flashcards for deck practice.

# Usage

Study English writing, words and sentences using a language pack inside Cognis Study.

## Prerequisites

An administrator must enable the language pack. Sign in and enroll in English through Study; dictionary and optional speech services require their own configuration.

## Examples

Select English in Study, open Explore and choose a layer. Open an entry to inspect its readings, meaning and related content; use Flashcards for deck practice.

Search an English word in the Study navigation, or use a vocabulary composer lookup. Same-composition readings and meanings are combined by Cognis. The API performs exact word lookup, not fuzzy/full-text suggestions or whole-sentence translation.

- Explore `/study/en/alphabet`, `/study/en/letter-combinations`, `/study/en/vocabulary` and `/study/en/sentences`.
- Search with Free Dictionary in the Study navigation; selected vocabulary results populate the host composer.
- Browse the English Alphabet, First English Words, Everyday English Verbs and First English Sentences decks under `/study/flashcards`. Cognis manages deck study, favourites and sharing.

### Use The Guide

Enroll in the language through Study settings. Visit its Explore page for the first-time guide, or choose the language guide from ? on the Study overview. That deliberate choice opens the language contents. Starting from Explore preserves the current layer. Completed guides only replay through ?.

Open the highlighted card to inspect its details, and choose Draw when available to try writing and its tools. Next skips branches you leave unopened. The creation branch opens the card-type chooser and editor; it may switch to a layer where cards can be created. No card is submitted automatically. Back, Restart and completion close tutorial surfaces while retaining normal discard confirmation.

## Expected Results

The selected layer displays provider content through the host Study interface. Available audio plays for its matching entry; unavailable optional synthesis does not prevent browsing.

## Troubleshooting

If English is absent, check module enablement and language enrollment. Dictionary failures require checking that service’s configuration; missing optional synthesis is not a failed language installation.
